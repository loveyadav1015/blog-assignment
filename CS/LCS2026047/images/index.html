Life at IIITL

Starting life as a new hostel student at IIIT Lucknow is a mix of many feelings. Every day brings something different. Some days are very tiring with back-to-back lectures and assignments. By evening, I just want to sleep. However, other days feel completely fresh and exciting, especially when I hang out with my new friends in the hostel late at night.

Honestly, the daily routine feels a lot like school. We have fixed timings, strict attendance, and regular classes from morning to evening. The mess food is also very average on most days. We eat simple dal, rice, and sabzi just to fill our stomachs. Because of this, everyone waits the whole week for Saturday lunch. That is when they serve hot chole bhature, and it easily feels like the best meal of the week!

What makes IIIT Lucknow truly special is the coding culture. After regular college hours, our seniors take technical classes on Competitive Programming (CP), Machine Learning (ML), and Open Source (FOSS). Learning directly from seniors is a unique factor here, and they teach very well. Even though college feels strict like school sometimes, these learning sessions and hostel memories make it great.
