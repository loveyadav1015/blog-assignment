Introduction

Until a few months ago, I could not make anything more complicated than instant noodles. Living away from home made me realise that cooking is a skill I should learn instead of postponing it forever. This blog is about the first full meal I cooked, the mistakes I made, and the small lessons I learned along the way.

Why I Decided to Cook

Eating outside every day became expensive and, honestly, a little boring. I also missed the taste of home food. When a friend told me that a simple meal needs only a few ingredients and about thirty minutes, I decided to try. I chose rice and dal because almost everyone I know says it is the easiest first dish.

My First Recipe

What I used

  • One cup of rice
  • Half a cup of yellow lentils
  • One onion and one tomato
  • Salt, turmeric, and a spoon of oil

How I made it

  1. Wash the rice and the lentils separately.
  2. Boil the rice in a pot until it turns soft.
  3. Cook the lentils with turmeric and salt in a pressure cooker.
  4. Fry the chopped onion and tomato in oil.
  5. Mix the fried masala into the lentils and let it simmer.

How Long Each Step Took

I kept a rough note of the time and the difficulty of every part of the meal:

Washing and chopping 10 minutes Easy

The frying was the hardest part for me because the onions burned in seconds. I read the basics on BBC Good Food and learned about heat control from the Wikipedia page on cooking.

Mistakes and Lessons

My rice was a little watery and the first batch of onions was black. I also forgot to add salt until the very end. These mistakes taught me to measure the water, to keep the flame low, and to taste the food while cooking. Every small error made the next attempt better.

Conclusion

Cooking my first meal taught me patience and showed me that any new skill becomes easier when it is broken into small steps. The food was not perfect, but I enjoyed every bite because I made it myself. Next, I want to try making rotis and a simple vegetable curry. If you have never cooked before, I strongly suggest that you start today.
