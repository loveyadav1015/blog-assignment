Looking Back at the Two-Year Grind

Now that the dust has finally settled and I'm finally seated in my room writing all these in this blog, I feel satisfied, accomplished and relaxed. The whole two years were totally something different which I dont think i'll ever experience in my life. Tha passion, hunger and kind of fear of knowledge and greed for a good college was something that made me going through it every single day. And to top it off, I was in the star batch of my coaching so U could NEVER imagine the hardships I've gone through. Anyways, now that I look back to all those grinds and all-nighters, It was totally worth it.

The journey was far from linear tho. I was just an ordinary student being pushed into a giant death pit, where everyone fought off to death (Ironically). I've had many ups and lows during this journey. But one thing about me was, well, I was very hardworking to the core.

My Subject-Wise Strategy Breakdown

Cracking an exam like JEE requires a balanced approach across all three core subjects. You cannot afford to leave any major gaps in your conceptual understanding. Here is the exact order of importance I assigned to my daily study blocks:

  1. Mathematics: Requires strong understanding and rigorous practice, luckly, my mathematics sir was really harsh so that was'nt an issue for me.
  2. Physics: Requires really good and deep concptual understanding of the subjects and their applications through countless examples. The most fun and intimidating subject for me personally.
  3. Chemistry: Tests ur memory retention skills (mostly organic chemistry) and numerical problem solving skills too (Physical chemistry). Many say those who likes physics will also like Physical chemistry.

The Golden Resources

While coaching modules form your core base, relying on authoritative reference books makes a massive difference in your problem-solving speed. If you are currently preparing, I highly recommend checking out these specific textbooks and platforms:

  • Mathematics: Coordinate Geometry and Calculus volumes by I.A. Maron and Cengage.
  • Physics: Concepts of Physics by Dr. H.C. Verma.
  • Chemistry: Organic Chemistry by Morrison & Boyd.
  • Official Portal: Always download past papers directly from the NTA Jee mains website to practice in the real exam layout.

Conclusion & Final Words of Wisdom

In conclusion, the Joint Entrance Examination is as much a test of your mental resilience as it is of your academic knowledge. Thousands of students possess the intelligence to crack it, but only those who can withstand the psychological pressure for two straight years make it to the other side.

If you are a future aspirant reading this, remember that one bad mock test does not define your potential. Stay disciplined, trust your teachers, and keep refining your mistakes. The view from the other side of the gate is absolutely worth the grind.
