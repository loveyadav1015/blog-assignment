Introduction

We use internet in our lives daily for different purposes like watching videos on YouTube, communicating via WhatsApp, using social media such as Instagram or Facebook, for educational purposes, shopping, business and what not. We think that everything is available on internet and in the matter of fact it is correct but it also includes things which should never be accessed. This hidden part of internet is called the Dark Web. It is filled with cybercrime, gore, illegal activities and many worse things.


  1. What Is Dark Web?

    2. Dark web is a part of internet that cannot be accessed directly with ordinary search engines like Brave, Chrome or Safari. A special browser called Tor Browser is used for it. The websites we use daily such as YouTube and Pinterest are included in the part of internet called the surface web. Basically, the surface web is the tip of the iceberg. Then comes deep web, which contains password-protected databases such as private emails and online banking. Then comes the dark web, the bottom of the iceberg. It is associated with lots of underground activities.

    Part of the Internet Examples Uses
    Surface Web YouTube, Google, Wikipedia Watching videos, searching for information, and browsing websites.
    Deep Web Private emails, online banking, password-protected databases Accessing private accounts and storing confidential information.
    Dark Web Tor-hosted websites, anonymous forums, hidden services Anonymous communication, privacy protection, and sometimes illegal activities.

    To know more about the dark web, surface web and deep web: Dark Web by Fing

  2. Uses of Dark Web

    • The dark web is accessed for privacy and anonymous communication. Tor technology was originally developed with U.S. Naval Research Laboratory involvement. It can also help journalists, activists and others communicate more privately.
    • However, some people misuse the dark web for activities such as cybercrime, illegal drug trading, hiring hitmen and stealing passwords or bank details.

  3. Risks and Dangers

    4. If you access the dark web without prior safety knowledge, you may be exposed to scams designed to hack your system and steal personal information. Malware, Trojans and viruses are potential risks. Disturbing and illegal content may also be encountered. Accessing or engaging with certain illegal material or activities could lead to legal trouble. Anonymity is not guaranteed on the dark web. Conclusion

The dark web is a hidden part of the internet that contains both useful and harmful information. It can be used for private communication, but it is also associated with illegal activities and cybercrime. Anyone using the internet should follow the law, remain aware of scammers and avoid sharing personal information carelessly.
