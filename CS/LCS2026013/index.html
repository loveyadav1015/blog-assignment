The impact of a fixed study routine

As a first year student, one thing i have felt so far is that success isn't all about smart, it's also about how you manage your time and maintain your routine . Initially, when i came into college, i thought i would study as per my mood, and i could score well, but as time passed , i could feel myself lagging behind other people, and i felt like i should make a proper routine which would keep me engaged and also i could study with more concentration. And , so far i can tell that this things works quite well. In this blog i'll share why this matters and how you can manage your time.

Why a study routine matters

A study routine helps in staying consistent and builds discipline. you can focus more on your work as compared to doing work at a completely random time. personally, i felt more calmer and confident when i follow my routine.

Important points while making a routine

  1. Set a fixed time for all days
  2. Make a to-do list
  3. Include short breaks
  4. Revise what you learned

Things that help

Apart form routine, a few habits can also make some differnce

  • Study in a quiet space
  • Drink enough water
  • get proper sleep
  • Keep learning new things

Some everyday problems

Problem Reason Solution
Procrastination Task feels difficult to start Begin with small amount of work and increase it regularly
Phone distractions Social Media and games Keep the phone away while studying
Forgetting what we studied Not revising topics regularly Spend some time revising what you studied
Exam stress Lack of preparations Regualar revision of topics
Poor time management No clear plan for day Make a to-do list everyday

Final thoughts

A good study routine isn't about being perfect. It's always about being consistent and disciplined. Some days will be hard and some will be smooth but you have to keep learning everyday. I assure you that having a routine is never gonna backfire
