Harry Potter

Introduction

Harry Potter is a fantasy series written by J.K. Rowling. It follows a young boy who discovers on his eleventh birthday that he is a wizard. The first book came out in 1997, and the series has been loved by readers around the world ever since.

The Four Houses of Hogwarts

At Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry, students are sorted into one of four houses:

  • Gryffindor - brave and daring
  • Hufflepuff - loyal and kind
  • Ravenclaw - clever and curious
  • Slytherin - ambitious and cunning

    • The Books in Order

  • Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone
  • Harry Potter and the Chamber of Secrets
  • Harry Potter and the Prisoner of Azkaban
  • Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire
  • Harry Potter and the Order of the Phoenix
  • Harry Potter and the Half-Blood Prince
  • Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows

    • Why It Matters

    The story is about friendship, courage, and choosing what is right, even when it is hard. Harry, Ron, and Hermione show that good friends can face almost anything together.

    Final Thoughts

    Whether you are reading the books for the first time or the tenth, there is always something new to notice. Which Hogwarts house would you be in?
