Introduction

Nature is an important part of our lives. Trees, rivers, mountains, animals, and plants make our planet beautiful. Nature provides us with fresh air, clean water, food, and many other resources. It also gives us peaceful places to relax and enjoy life

Benefits of Nature

Nature supports life on Earth. Trees absorb carbon dioxide and release oxygen. Rivers and lakes provide water, while plants and animals maintain the balance of ecosystems. Spending time in green spaces can also help people feel calm and refreshed.

How We Can Protect Nature

  1. Plant more trees.
  2. Save water and electricity.
  3. Avoid plastic usage.
  4. Keep our surroundings clean.
  5. Avoid cutting trees.

Conclusion

Nature is a precious gift that we must protect. Small actions, such as planting trees, saving water, and reducing waste, can make a difference. If everyone takes responsibility for the environment, we can create a cleaner and healthier planet for future generations.
