The City of Nawabs

Lucknow, the capital of Uttar Pradesh, is often called the City of Nawabs. It is famous for its historic monuments, refined etiquette, traditional crafts, and distinctive Awadhi cuisine.

History and Heritage

Lucknow flourished under the Nawabs of Awadh, especially during the eighteenth and nineteenth centuries. Their influence can still be seen in the city's grand buildings, gardens, music, poetry, and cultural traditions.

  1. Begin at Bara Imambara and admire its impressive architecture.
  2. Visit the nearby Rumi Darwaza, a famous gateway and city landmark.
  3. End at Chota Imambara to see its ornate interiors and historic surroundings.

Food, Language and Culture

Lucknow is celebrated for its polite way of speaking, Urdu poetry, chikankari embroidery, and welcoming culture. Its food reflects the rich flavours of Awadhi cooking.

  • Try Awadhi biryani or the famous Galouti kebab.
  • Explore chikankari embroidery, a traditional craft associated with Lucknow.
  • Ask before photographing people or shops, and explore local markets respectfully.
