Why Discuss this?

In the modern world, more than ever we stand and grapple with complex moral problems and quandries. These problems are only further accentuated by the advent of Artificial Intelligence and other technology that is capable of both immense good and potentially pain.

Etics refers to a system of principles or moral precepts that one chooses to coopt. It is in other words a science or philosophy of what is right and what is wrong.

But different people have different opinions on whether this is a science or a philosophy, whether the system of ethics itself is grounded in discovering some objective pinrciples or facts like the laws of physics or discovering merely subjective experiences akin to philosophy or spirituality. This difference in approach is the core crux of metaethics, or the foundational base of ethics itself.

So there were two systems of metaethics:-

  1. Moral Objectivism
  2. Moral antirealism

Moral Objectivism

This is the system of morality or metaethics advocated by many ancient philosophers, including plato and aristotle. Moral Objectivism believes in morality or ethics as a science, where there are some definite moral truths. MOral objectivism or Moral realism, basically postulates that there are some definite moral goods and definite moral bads. This is the sort of moral system that is most common even amongst us in a way. For example:- Those who oppose capital punishment no matter the crime, believe so because for them it is a moral fact that people have the sacred right to life irrespective of the crime they commit. Similarly, some people believe staunchly that marriage is a lifelong endeavour with no divoce or no contract. As such, moral facts essentially are permanent, eternal truths in this system like how the Sun rising in the east is a fact. This view despite being common has some obvious flaws. If in fact somethings are objective moral facts, what would explain different cultural notions towards this? If killing people is inherently wrong, then does capital punishment of a terrorist become a sin? If nurturing children is an inherently good thing, should children indoctrinated with extremist ideologies and those who have killed people, be nurtured in the same way? Moral Objectivism fails to account for the difference of Moral facts, as in for example, the fact that in some cultures marriage is merely a contract and not a lifelong setup. As per moral objectivism, once a moral fact has been discoverd, all contradicting opinions are essentially deviations or wrong in a way and moral progress is making everyone follow this correct moral fact. This seems to lead to a slipper slope to xenophobia and cultural superiority that coerces other people to listen to what your culture or belief system suggests and suppress any dissent. There are other people who say there may be multiple moral objective truths but even then the fact that they believe these things to be facts and not subjective feelings or cultural notions, quite disctinctly contradicts with real life and people's lived experiences that can cause valid people to have completely invalid and contrasting views.

Moral Antirealism

This is the system that essentially says there is no system of ethics that is inherently objective. Morality is what people think to be moral based on their notions, cultural conventions and beliefs. Moral Antirealism in a way expands the scope to allow for any society to have it's own moral code. This may seem like a path to have inclusive, tolerant, multicultural societies. However, it eliminates all grounding values in totality. This means that every culture as it is is considered perfect or suffieicnet and there is no scope for anything we could describe as moral progress. Because Moral antirealism has no grounding in some core principles, if a moral antirealist lived during the Nazi era, for him Nazi culture focused on the extermination and othering of the Jews would merely be a culture to be respected and not something to be attacked as morally 'bad' because there is no bad. Moral relativism hence leads us to the other end of the slippery slope, where there is complete elimination itself of good and bad, and people do whatever they wish and they are good as long as they can get people in their culture or society to get behind them.

System Basis Advocates Example
Moral Objectivism Objective Moral Facts Plato,Aristotle,etc Human life is sacred
Moral antirealism Subjective Moral Notions Mackie,Ayer,etc Nothing is inherently wrong.
Conclusion

It seems to me, in my personal opinion that a synthesis of these two views is required. Where we accept, that morality is a lot of subjectiveness inherently and different cultures can have different values but also believe in a particular few facts or values or grounding statements. If we base off our moral systems on core tenets or valuable real life values like Objectivism can allow us to do, and these may be held supreme across all cultures, it would be the best way while allowing cultures leeway in adopting their own conventions and notions in other areas.


In other words, believe in Moral Objectivism that's ready to understand the moral facts of other cultures. I would advocate to have for ourselves some moral objective facts and avoid antirealism, but accept the fallibility of our own values and others, to be ready to always advocate for change.
