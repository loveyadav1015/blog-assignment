FT. COLLEGE LIFE 🎓

One wants us to become an engineer. The other wants us to become a travel influencer!

🤣 Welcome to Every Indian College Student's Life!

Welcome to the battlefield where two powerful teams fight every single day:

👨‍👩‍👦 TEAM PARENTS vs 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 TEAM FRIENDS

Parents: "Beta, college jaakar bas padhai karna. Humne tumhe engineer banane bheja hai!"

Friends: "Bhai, attendance 75% ho gayi? Chalo ab Manali trip plan karte hain!"

Meanwhile, the student is sitting on the bed thinking:

"Mummy ko lagta hai main IIT Bombay mein research kar raha hoon. Doston ko lagta hai main Goa ka tourism minister hoon!" 😂

Let's compare these two completely different mindsets!

👨‍👩‍👦 TEAM PARENTS: "PADHAI KARO, LIFE SET HAI!" 📚

Parents send us to college with one simple dream: Study hard, get good marks, get a good placement, and make the family proud.

Their expectations are simple. Our problems are complicated.

1. When Parents Call 📞

Mom: Beta, kya kar rahe ho?

Student: Bas mummy, padh raha hoon.

Actual Situation: Lying on the bed, watching reels, with a packet of chips in one hand and the phone in the other.

Mom: Achha, padhai karo. Phone kam chalaya karo!

Student: Haan mummy, bilkul! 😂

2. Parents' Definition of a Perfect College Day 📖

  1. Wake up at 6 AM.
  2. Attend every lecture.
  3. Take notes seriously.
  4. Revise everything after college.
  5. Complete assignments before the deadline.
  6. Sleep early and repeat.

Parents think college is a place where students study for eight hours daily.

Reality: Eight hours of sleep, two hours of scrolling, three hours of chilling, and fifteen minutes of studying before the exam.

3. The Placement Pressure 💼

Dad: Beta, package kitna lagega?

Student: Papa, abhi toh first semester hai!

Dad: Haan toh? Sharma ji ke bete ne toh first year mein hi coding shuru kar di thi!

Suddenly, the student opens a coding tutorial and watches the first 30 seconds.

Student: Bas papa, ab toh Google mein hi jaaunga! 💻😂

4. Parents' Greatest Fear 😭

The greatest fear is not that we will fail an exam. It is that we will waste our college life.

Their dream: A successful engineer.

Their nightmare: An engineer who knows every cafe in the city but cannot explain what is taught in class!

🧑‍🤝‍🧑 TEAM FRIENDS: "BHAI, LIFE EK HI BAAR MILTI HAI!" 🥳

Friends have a completely different syllabus. Their syllabus includes trips, food, movies, late-night talks, gaming, and bunking lectures.

1. The Legendary Trip Plan 🏔️

Friend 1: Bhai, Manali chalte hain!

Friend 2: Haan bhai, pakka!

Friend 3: Budget kitna hai?

Friend 1: ₹500 each.

Friend 2: Bhai, ₹500 mein toh hostel se railway station tak ka auto bhi nahi aayega!

Friend 3: Koi na bhai, pehle Google Maps pe trip kar lete hain! 😂

Final Result: The trip gets cancelled, but everyone orders pizza worth ₹1,200.

2. The Bunking Strategy 🏃

Friend: Bhai, aaj lecture bunk karte hain?

You: Attendance 76% hai bhai!

Friend: Tension mat le, life mein risk lena chahiye!

Five minutes later, both are sitting in the canteen.

One hour later, the professor announces an unexpected attendance shortage notice.

Friendship ended. Attendance started. 💀

3. The Midnight Maggi Club 🍜

At 2 AM, one friend suddenly becomes a chef.

Friend: Bhai, Maggi khaayega?

You: Bhai, kal exam hai!

Friend: Toh? Maggi khaane se knowledge badhti hai!

Eight packets of Maggi later, nobody has studied, but everyone is emotionally satisfied.

4. Friends' Financial Management 💸

Monday: "Bhai, treat meri taraf se!"

Tuesday: "Bhai, ₹100 UPI kar de. Kal return karta hoon."

Wednesday: "Bhai, Google Pay down hai kya?"

Thursday: "Bhai, tu mera sachha dost hai na?"

Friday: "Bhai, weekend trip pakki?"

Bank balance: ₹12.50

Confidence level: BILLIONAIRE. 😂

⚔️ THE ULTIMATE COMPARISON

Situation Parents' Reaction Friends' Reaction
Exam Tomorrow Study the entire syllabus. Bro, send important questions!
Free Weekend Revise your notes. GOA TRIP? 🏖️
Low Marks Where did we go wrong? Bro, the professor has personal issues!
New Semester This time, study seriously. New semester, new bunking records.
Monthly Expenses Save money. Order one more pizza!
Career Plans Prepare for placements. Bro, let's start a startup!
Sunday Morning Wake up early. Bro, breakfast at 2 PM?
Coming Home Come home soon! Bro, one last night-out!

🤡 THE STUDENT: STUCK BETWEEN TWO WORLDS

Parents want us to study. Friends want us to chill. Professors want assignments. The college wants attendance. The bank account wants money. And we just want eight hours of uninterrupted sleep.

The Daily Struggle

  • Morning: Promise parents that you will study hard.
  • Afternoon: Go to the canteen with friends.
  • Evening: Plan a trip that nobody can afford.
  • Night: Open the books.
  • 11 PM: Watch one YouTube video.
  • 2 AM: Discover that the exam is tomorrow.
  • 3 AM: "Bhai, important questions bhej!"

And somehow, we survive every semester!

College is not a degree. It is a survival game! 🎮

🏆 FINAL VERDICT: WHO WINS?

If the competition is about studying, parents win.

If the competition is about chilling, friends win.

If the competition is about spending money, somehow the student loses. 😂

But jokes apart, parents work hard to give us a better future, while friends make our present unforgettable.

College life is about finding the balance between studying for our dreams and enjoying the journey.

"Study like your parents are watching. Chill like your friends are planning. Live like college ends tomorrow!" ❤️

Because one day, these crazy college memories will become the stories we tell our children!
