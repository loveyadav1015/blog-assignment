My First Experience at IIIT Lucknow

Introduction

Good morning everyone! My name is Gaddale Tanuja. I am from Mahabubnagar , Telangana . I am a first-year B.Tech student in the Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence (CSAI) branch at IIIT Lucknow. My Enrollment number is LCI2026041.

My First Day at College

My first day at IIIT Lucknow was super exciting because I was in front of my dream college. I was so excited to start my college journey. I met new friends, new people, explored the campus, got to know about few things , college culture , and I was excited to go to classes after the orientation.

My Academic Journey

As a CSAI student, I am learning programming languages like C and C++. I am also interested in learning HTML, web development, and artificial intelligence. As I am a beginner in coding , there are alot of things I have to learn . Btech is not easy as I thought . I have to work hard. Not only in academics I also participated in many events conducted by seniors in the college . It was really fun to take part in events . There is also a cultural club in our college and I am thrilled to take part of that . Also enjoyed our Fresher's day .

My Goals

  4. Become a good software engineer
  5. Building practical and project portfolios
  6. Participate in hackathons

Things I Like About College

  • Learning programming
  • Exploring the campus

Conclusion

My journey at IIIT Lucknow has just started. And I am excited to explore new opportunities , gain knowledge and develop new skills .I look forward to making wonderful memories, building friendships and overcoming challenges. I hope this journey will help me grow academically. I hope to learn many new things, and achieve my goals, shaping a bright future ahead.
