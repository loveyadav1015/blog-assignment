Challenges India Must Overcome

Despite significant progress, India faces several challenges in technological development. These include unequal access to the internet, a shortage of advanced research facilities in some areas, cybersecurity risks, and the need for better technical education.

To overcome these challenges, India must invest in research and development, improve digital infrastructure, support scientists and engineers, and provide quality technical education to students.
