INFORMATION ABOUT CRICKET

Cricket is a famous bat-and-ball team sport played by two teams of eleven players on an open field with a 22-yard central pitch. One team bats to score as many runs as possible, while the opposing team bowls and fields to get the batters out. The game is played in various formats—including long Test matches, One Day Internationals (ODIs), and fast-paced T20 matches—and is governed globally by the International Cricket Council.

In countries like India, cricket is more than just a game; it is a massive cultural passion deeply woven into everyday life. From historic World Cup victories to children playing in neighborhood streets, the sport builds physical fitness, patience, and strong teamwork.

INFORMATION ABOUT MUSIC

Music is an art form that combines vocal or instrumental sounds using rhythm, melody, and harmony to express human emotions and ideas. People create it through singing or playing instruments like guitars, pianos, and drums. It has existed across all human cultures since prehistoric times as a universal language that crosses cultural and linguistic boundaries

Beyond entertainment, music plays a deep role in human life, culture, and health. It helps reduce stress, calm anxiety, and uplift moods while bringing people together through concerts, dances, and shared traditions. From classical symphonies and folk songs to modern pop and rock, music gives voice to feelings that simple words cannot describe.

INFORMATION ABOUT CODING

Coding is the process of writing step-by-step instructions that tell a computer how to perform specific tasks or solve problems. Developers translate human-readable ideas into structured logic using specialized Programming Languages like Python, Java, C++, and JavaScript. These written commands—known as source code—serve as a recipe or algorithm that guides the machine through logic flows, loops, and conditional decisions.

The primary purpose of coding is to build the digital tools and infrastructure that power modern society, including websites, mobile applications, operating systems, and video games. Through low-level binary code or high-level frameworks, coding bridges the gap between human intent and machine execution. It requires logical thinking, pattern recognition, and continuous debugging to ensure software runs smoothly and reliably
