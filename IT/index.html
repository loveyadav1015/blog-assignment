The Harsh Reality

Living in a college hostel is often sold as the ultimate dream of freedom. You think you will stay up all night, eat whatever you want, and have endless fun without your parents watching. However, the reality hits you when you enter a small shared room. This is a basic guide to surviving the weird world of living in a student hostel.

First, let us talk about the illusion of personal space. When you share a room, your personal space is life furrrr. Everything is common territory. Your roommate will borrow your things without asking, set alarms for 5:00 AM only to wake me up, and eat the snacks your mother packed for you. It is a true test of human patience. But somehow, you learn to adapt and even become best friends with the person who drives you crazy.

The Unwritten Rules

To survive, you must learn the unwritten rules of the hostel. There is no official rulebook and warder is also not the one making rules, its the student that makes rules there and follow.

The Golden Laws

  1. The Snack Tax: If you open a packet of chips in the front of everyone then everyone gets a share. You cannot eat alone. And they eat it like janwar.
  2. Laundry is an Illusion: You will start the semester washing your clothes weekly. By the end, you will just smell them to see if they can survive one more day.
  3. Headphones are Mandatory: Nobody wants to hear your terrible music taste at midnight.

Survival Things

You cannot rely on the hostel mess for good food or comfort, so you have to be prepared. Below is a simple table showing the most critical items you must bring with you to survive the semester.

Electric Kettle Making maggi midnight Critical

Conclusion

In conclusion, hostel life is terrifying and chaotic, but it is also the best time of your life. The mess food might be awful, and the rooms might be small, but the memories you make will last forever. You learn how to be independent, how to share your space, and how to sleep through extremely loud music. It is crazy , but you will start missing them when it is over.
