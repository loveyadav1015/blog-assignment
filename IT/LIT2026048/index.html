When My Smartphone Becomes My Worst Enemy

If my smartphone is my best friend , it is also my worst enemy in many cases one of which is acads.The problem begins when one useful check about update regarding classes turns into an hour of scrolling. The main thing that takes my whole attention sometimes is notification, especially when I am trying to focus on a problem. When I was preparing for the jee exam , at that time i do not use instagram at that time but at that time youtube shorts was my biggest distraction. But since i joined college , i started using instagram which is a much bigger distraction than youtube. Social media sometime makes me compare my ordinary day with someone else's best moments. Besides it, Many times i keep watching reels for 2 hours when i should be sleeping, some or many times i compromise my sleep to just watch reels and that leads to affect my schedule. These risks do not mean that phones are bad. They mean that I need to decide when the phone is helping me and when it is controlling me.

Trapped By Smartphone

Habits That I Will Change Definitely

  • Keeping the phone away from my desk during focused study.
  • Turning off notifications that are not important.
  • Avoiding screens for a while before sleeping.
  • Talking to people in front of me before checking a message.

My Simple Plan To Use My Smartphone Wisely

I do not want to remove my smartphone from my life just to avoid distractions; I want to use it by my choice according to my needs. The following plan gives useful tasks a place in my day and protects time for rest and physical activities rather than playing online games. There is no compulsion for me to follow the timetable strictly, but the idea is to avoid opening apps that will waste my precious time without any reason.

PART OF DAY PURPOSE OF MOBILE USAGE LIMIT THAT I WILL FOLLOW
Morning Alarm, timetable, and update messages from announcement group No social media before breakfast
Study time Research and class material Use focus mode for 45 minutes
Evening Family calls and entertainment Stop one hour before sleep

For another perspective, I can read the World Health Organization information on adolescent mental health and think about which habits protect my well-being.

CONCLUSION

My smartphone is neither my best friend nor my worst enemy. It becomes my friend when it helps me learn, Focus on my Day, find my way, and stay in touch with my parents and my family I care about. It becomes my enemy when due to it, I have to compromise my sleep, sometimes it takes away my whole attention span, and steals my confidence about everything i am good at. The most important skill is choosing how and when to use the phone. By setting certain limits and keeping my goals in mind, and focusing on them also can make my smartphone serve my life and help me to live a life that is easy with the use of smartphone instead of allowing it to control it.
