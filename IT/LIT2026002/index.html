About My College Life

College life is a whole new experience for me. Entering college feels like getting freedom and stepping into a bigger world. Even though it has only been a month, I meet new people every day and see many new faces. In the beginning, managing lectures, completing assignments on time, and attending campus events was a bit hard, but I am slowly getting used to it.

One of the best parts of my week is working in the computer labs and attending technical classes. Every class teaches me something new. Some topics can be hard to understand at first, but practicing regularly and studying with friends makes learning enjoyable.

Things I Like About Coding

Coding feels like solving a new puzzle every single day. Here are a few simple reasons why I enjoy practicing it:

  1. It teaches logical and step-by-step thinking.
  2. It allows me to build real web pages on my own computer.
  3. There are always free resources available whenever I get stuck.

Whenever I want to read extra notes, I visit Wikipedia or search tutorials on Google to clear my basic doubts quickly.
