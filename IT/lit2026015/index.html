The FINALS '17'

The First Exposure

It was the cold evening of Jan 2017 when I means Mr Aaditya Jee, a small child lying in his bed with no backlog ,no pending topics, nothing just a mind free from everything, was watching random things on my television , when suddenly my elder brother came into the room grabbed the remote from me and changed the channel to sports.

BUT WHY??

It was because YUVRAJ SINGH was hitting sixes over sixes,and was in 90s and my brother wanted to saw his century.My reaction was what is this ? Looking like wow , that thing was amazing and after the completion of the century my brother returned me the remote and went out. But the child inside me wanted to explore what was that thing. That day i came to know that the drama i did every evening in the ground outside my house was not cricket. The actual cricket is what i had seen in the tv where countries play among themselves on a big scale. Btw it was India against England One day internationals match that i had seen completely.

For the scorecard visit: IND vs ENG

Yuvraj Singh had scored 150 in that match and i became a big fan of him from that day,smashing fours and sixes to Liam Plunkett and Chris Woakes, what a beautiful inning that was!!!!

Day kept on going and i kept on checking every evening was the same thing repeating or not and after every 2 3 days it witnessed that again beacuse it was a 5 match series. But how a small child know about that much so the knowledge inside me about cricket kept increasing slowly.

The ICC tournament

After some months , came the ICC tournament the Champions Trophy,I watched almost every match till India reached finals.

It was sunday and the finals was Ind against Pak. I studied all day from early morning to the afternoon just for the sake of watching the match. I told my mother i have studied all day so please allow me to watch television tonight and she agreed.

The match had started at 3 pm on sunday and Ind was bowling first.And in the very beginning Bumrah dismissed Fakhar Zamaan on 3 but unfortunately it was an overstepped no ball, and after that the beast inside Fakhar punished Ind with a furious Century in the ground of The Oval. I went to play cricket after that because i was not able to tolerate the rain of boundaries against Ind that too by Pak.
