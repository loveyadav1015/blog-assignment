FEW RECORDS

The Begining


Every legend has a beginning, and Virat Kohli's story began with a tragedy that no one wants to face.Born into a middle-class family in Delhi, he wasn't handed greatness on a silver platter. He was a young boy with a bat in his hands, a fire in his eyes, and a dream that was bigger than his circumstances. Cricket wasn't just a passion for him it was a commitment he was willing to build his life around. The loss of his father in 2006, while he was still an emerging cricketer, tested him at an age when most boys were still figuring out life. Yet, he returned to the crease. From the dusty grounds of Delhi to captaining India to the Under-19 World Cup title in 2008. Nobody gave him the throne. He fought for every inch of it. And perhaps that's where the story of King Kohli truly began.

Chase Master

Then came the peak — the era when chasing targets became Virat Kohli’s kingdom. From his breathtaking 183 against Pakistan to dismantling Australia’s bowling in Jaipur, he made impossible targets look ordinary. When wickets fell and pressure mounted, Kohli stood tall. He didn't just chase runs; he chased victory with a hunger that refused to surrender.
