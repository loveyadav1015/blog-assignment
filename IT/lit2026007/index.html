Indian Institute of Information Technology

Overview

It is a nation public private partnership based college. It is one of the 26 Indian Institution of Information Technology of India.

How to get enrolled in this institute?

To get enrolled in this prestigious college you will have to gain a good rank in JEE mains examinations and come under the cut-off ranks offered for the colleges under the JOSSA counselling.

Perks of being a IIIT Lucknow student

If you are a student of IIIT Lucknow then you will get to experience one of the best coding culture of india and a very friendly strong bonds with seniors and juniors.

Cons of being a IIIT Lucknow student

You get no infrasture and no proper playgrounds and no in campus hostel in first year.
