Time Management in collage

Problem with time management in collage

The problem with time management in college is that there are lots of things you want to do and try, but due to a lack of time, you are not able to do too many things. Due to poor time management skills, you end up starting everything but not doing anything consistently. You even fall short in the things you must do, like CP.

Why time management

Time management is a crucial skill to learn in college. If we don't learn it, we will either miss out on many things or fail to do what we are actually here for. It is one of those skills that we must learn.

How to improve time management skills

First, write down all your tasks and arrange them in order of priority. Then, complete the tasks that take less than five minutes. Finishing these small tasks will boost our confidence and motivate us to tackle the remaining tasks. If we leave them unfinished, they will only add to our mental burden.

conclusion

whatever you may do, learn atleast time management. We are in college. This is our battleground. We will try and fail, but we will learn along the way.
