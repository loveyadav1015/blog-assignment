What Are Asteroids?

Asteroids are small rocky objects that move around the Sun. Most of them are found between Mars and Jupiter. They are leftovers from the formation of our solar system.

Learn more about asteroids

Some Interesting Facts

  • Asteroids come in different sizes.
  • Some asteroids have their own moons.
  • Scientists study them to learn about space.

Famous Asteroids

  2. Vesta
  3. Eros

Conclusion

I find asteroids interesting because they help us understand how our solar system formed. Studying them also helps scientists learn more about the risks they may pose to Earth.
